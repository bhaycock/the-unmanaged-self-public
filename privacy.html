The Unmanaged Self: privacy policy

Updated 4 October 2026

The Unmanaged Self is published by Barry Haycock (BaZ). For privacy questions or requests, email dubdates.app@gmail.com.

Your writing

Your journal, reflections, feelings, pattern marks and the Mirror are kept in an encrypted file on your iPhone. A key held by this device's Keychain protects that file. We do not receive your writing, listen to recordings from you, or send journal content to an AI service. There are no user accounts, advertisements or third-party analytics in this iOS preview.

The optional journal lock uses your device's authentication. iOS handles Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode; the app receives an authentication result. Biometric data is not provided to us. The lock is separate from the encryption key, so changing your device biometrics does not destroy your journal.

The app excludes its private journal files from device backup, and the Keychain key stays on this device. Deleting the app removes its local writing and progress. We cannot recover a copy for you.

Your own PDF copy

You can choose to export a readable journal PDF that also contains structured journal entries and course progress for restoring them later, including in the Android app. A password is optional. The destination you choose may be on your phone or a cloud storage provider. Exporting is your choice: that copy leaves the app and is governed by the destination you select. Anyone able to open an unprotected copy can read it. Keep your password and backup safe; we cannot recover a forgotten PDF password. Restoring a PDF reads it on your phone and adds missing writing without replacing your existing choices.

Course and purchases

The introduction and Days 1 to 3 are bundled in this iOS preview. Days 4 to 28, purchases and downloads are not available in this build. This iOS preview does not contact our purchase verification service or send listening progress or journal content to Supabase. The privacy policy will be updated before those features are released.

Permissions and choices

The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Background sound plays only while the app is in front, if you leave that setting on. Karl's recordings can continue when the app is in the background. The app does not send notifications to bring you back.

Deleting the app does not delete a PDF you exported. Manage exported copies at their chosen destination. Please do not send journal content or payment card details when contacting us.

Adults and changes

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is guided reflection, not therapy or crisis care. We will update this policy when data handling changes.
