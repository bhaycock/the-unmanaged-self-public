The Unmanaged Self: Android privacy policy

Updated 5 October 2026

The Unmanaged Self is published by Barry Haycock (BaZ). For privacy questions or requests, email dubdates.app@gmail.com.

Your writing

Your journal, reflections, feelings, pattern marks and the Mirror are kept in an encrypted database on your phone. Android Keystore protects the database key. We do not receive your writing, listen to recordings from you, or send journal content to an AI service. There are no user accounts, advertisements or third-party analytics.

The optional journal lock uses your device's authentication. Android handles your fingerprint, face or device credential; the app receives an authentication result. Biometric data is not provided to us.

The app excludes its private data from Android backup and device transfer. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes your journal and progress from the phone. We cannot recover a copy for you.

Your own PDF copy

You can choose to export a readable journal PDF that also contains structured journal entries and course progress for restoring them later. A password is optional. The destination you choose may be on your phone or a cloud storage provider. Exporting is your choice: that copy leaves the app and is governed by the destination you select. Anyone able to open an unprotected copy can read it. Keep your password and backup safe; we cannot recover a forgotten PDF password. Restoring a PDF reads it on your phone.

Purchases and downloads

Google Play handles payment and purchase history. We do not receive your card details. When checking or restoring a purchase, the app sends the Google Play purchase token, a hash of this installation's public key and the app version to our verification service. The service checks the purchase with Google, acknowledges it and issues a signed certificate for offline access. The app keeps purchase tokens encrypted on the phone, including a completed purchase waiting for verification after an interruption.

Our database stores hashes of purchase tokens and installation keys, the package and product identifiers, purchase and acknowledgement state, a purchase category such as test or ordinary, and verification, receipt and revocation timestamps. Purchase verification records are currently retained indefinitely to support permanent purchase restoration and refund handling. You can contact us to request deletion or ask about this data. We may need purchase information to locate the correct record; do not send journal content or payment card details.

Supabase hosts verification and private course files in a European region. Download requests include the purchase token and installation-key hash. The hosting providers process network information such as IP addresses, request times and requested files to operate their services. These technical logs follow the providers' retention settings. We do not send listening progress or journal content in these requests. Google processes purchases and distribution under its own policies.

The introduction and Days 1 to 3 are bundled. Paid files download after verification and remain on the phone for offline use. Purchase checks can run when the app comes to the foreground and while an interrupted verification is being retried. A missing connection does not cancel existing verified offline access. A confirmed refund or revocation can remove access to paid sessions, but does not remove journal entries or course progress.

Permissions and choices

Internet and network state support purchases and downloads. Foreground services support background playback and downloads. Download notifications show progress when notification permission is granted. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access. You can remove downloaded course files while keeping your purchase and writing, and download them again later.

Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not delete a PDF you exported, your Google Play purchase history or server verification records. Manage exported copies at their chosen destination and contact us about server records. We will assess access, correction and deletion requests and explain any records that must be retained.

Adults and changes

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is guided reflection, not therapy or crisis care. We will update this policy when data handling changes.
